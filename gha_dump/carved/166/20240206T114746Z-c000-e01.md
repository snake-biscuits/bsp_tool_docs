## `wave_height`
`66.0` & `-8.0` on Storm Point
`bounds.mins.z` & `bounds.maxs.z` is `-8.0`
So I figure it's range the heights allowed for waves (above the surface of meshes within bounds)

`mins.z` is always the same as `wave_height.bottom`
But 1 body has a different height for each map
`storm_point[0] = 66.0`, otherwise `0.0`
`habitat[1] = 3328.0`, otherwise `1857.0`

## `rpak_hash`
`uint64_t`
@r-ex connected this to `materials/world/ocean_water` & other related textures
A different asset hash for each body, might take some digging to figure out how it's used exactly

## `uv_scale`
Small float representing the amount to increment a uv tile across the 1K `WaveData` texture
`1024` * `step_size` represents how many pixels across a tile is (might also be some border amount to avoid bleed)

## `tile_scale`
Always `1024.0`, the x & y dimension of a tile in the mesh
