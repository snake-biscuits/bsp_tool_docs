The way `BrushSideProperty.UNKNOWN_FLAG` appears here makes me think it's used on faces that abut other brushes
(and would never be touched / seen)
Ignoring physics on these sides could limit "pixel walking"

Pixel walking occurs when a rounding error allows a player to stand on the unexposed of a brush, whilst against a wall
