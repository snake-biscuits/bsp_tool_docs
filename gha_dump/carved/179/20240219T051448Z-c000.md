To index `BrushSides` you need to keep track of those indexed by previous `Brushes`
In `respawn.titanfall` `CMBrush` has some members to keep track of this

Axial sides do not have planes (just like `respawn.titanfall`)
The `plane` `union` switches between `float` plane distance & `uint32_t` plane index for axial & non-axial sides.

Comparing the `mp/mp_lobby.d3dbsp` test map to `mp_lobby.map` seems to indicate the following axial order:
 * `-X` `+X` `-Y` `+Y` `-Z` `+Z`

Haven't 100% confirmed this, but it seems to line up
Brush & side order isn't the same between the `.d3dbsp` & `.map`
