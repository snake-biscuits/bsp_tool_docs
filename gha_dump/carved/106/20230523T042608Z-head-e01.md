TriColl lumps are used in `respawn.titanfall` & `respawn.titanfall2`
Presumably compiled from patches, `misc_model` & other non-brush geo
In Titanfall, most Tricoll is used for terrain, other examples include curved building exteriors on Runoff
In Titanfall 2, maps are mostly made of TriColl (curved surfaces, more terrain. complex bespoke geo)

Attempting to port Titanfall maps to Titanfall 2, we have found crashes when players touch TriColl geo
The logs indicate some out of bounds indexing
All we know for sure if that the lumps have changed.

### Lumps
```python
TRICOLL_TRIANGLES = 0x0042
TRICOLL_NODES = 0x0044
TRICOLL_HEADERS = 0x0045
TRICOLL_BEVEL_STARTS = 0x0060
TRICOLL_BEVEL_INDICES = 0x0061
```
