As part of their internal commit & build systems at respawn, they used [Jenkins](https://www.jenkins.io/) to generate `.stbsp`

This work was dispatched to their "Precomputation Cruncher", pictured below:
![stbsp_gpu_cruncher](https://github.com/snake-biscuits/bsp_tool/assets/36507175/71835f04-68fa-4e0e-80ba-3896a8dff71f)

From what I understand of the GDC talk, they would do a histogram of texture scale relative to a "probe"
Essentially a virtual player that looks at the map & decides what texture resolution looks best

`.stbsp` files seem to store the results of these histograms in some way, indexing specific material names
Mostly targetting `.rpak` materials, though some `.vmt` support exists
