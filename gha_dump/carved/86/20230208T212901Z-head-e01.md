## Straddle Groups
 - `0` unused?
 - `1` `Brushes[0 & 3]` (+/- Z)
   * `GridCells[0..3]`
 - `2` `Brushes[1]` (+Y)
   * `GridCells[2 & 3]`
 - `3` `Brushes[2]` (+X)
   * `GridCells[1 & 3]`
 - `4` `Brushes[4]` (-Y)
   * `GridCell[0 & 1]`
 - `5` `Brushes[5]` (-X)
   * `GridCell[0 & 2]`
