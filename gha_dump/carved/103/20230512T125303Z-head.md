We've been collecting older seasons of Apex to help with reverse engineering
This helps us track:
 * format changes
 * map releases (& when they're added to the game early)
 * probably a third thing but i forgor :skull:
 
 ## Warning
 **downloading all `.vpk`s can easily take up 60+ GB PER UPDATE!**
 **make sure you have enough drive space before downloading!!**
 **and don't forget about your internet costs either!!!**

## Tools
[DepotDownloader](https://github.com/SteamRE/DepotDownloader/releases/tag/DepotDownloader_2.4.7)
```bash
DepotDownloader.exe -app 1172470 -depot 1172471 -manifest <ID> -filelist filelist.txt -username <steam username>`
```
`filelist.txt`:
```
regex:vpk/.*\.vpk
```
You will be prompted to enter your steam login (will be censored w/ `*` as you type)
Have your phone nearby to enter Steam Guard codes too

> NOTE: steam username is not your steam nickname, must be account login!

regex for a specific map: `vpk/.*_mp_rr_canyonlands.*\.vpk`
catches `client_*.bsp.pak000_00X.vpk` & `englishclient_*.bsp.pak000_dir.vpk`
