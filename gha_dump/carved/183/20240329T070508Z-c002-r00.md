Looks like id tech 3 deluxemaps to me. (The term is kind of weird imo. Sourceports only material ofc.) It's bascially storing the average light incident vector, so one can use normal mapping on lightmapped surfaces. Judging from the colors the vector is stored in worldspace not in tangentspace.

The other possibility is it's a bent normal map, which is indeed used often for GI implementations. This would basically tell the game where to sample from from a lighting volume like an spherical harmonics lightgrid.

From a quick glance though I'd guess its a "deluxemap". Lightspots (from the lightmaps above) create this sphere shape in this one, which is typical for this kind of map.
