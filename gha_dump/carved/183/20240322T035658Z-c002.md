## `HDR.C`
This is where the similarities with the Titanfall Engine begin
Looks like a normal map, though it's not a 1:1 copy of the surface normal
Just like w/ Titanfall's `SKY.B`, I assume this is for bounced lighting (Global Illumination)

Doesn't look like the Alpha channel is used for Exponent, but I could be wrong
