## `HDR.A` & `HDR.B`
It looks like Nexon increased the potential Dynamic Range by splitting the original Lightmap into 2 ranges
Would explain why early attempts to port face lightmaps resulted in visible banding in-game
> These early attempts were done by Organnerx & other CSO:2 modders
> Building on the CS:S -> CS:O 2 conversion tool: [~leite/cso2-bsp-converter](https://git.sr.ht/~leite/cso2-bsp-converter)
