> Lightmap Pages can be assembled at runtime to save on video memory

The Source Engine isn't great at assembling these pages
No consideration is taken for texture bleed
Bleed can be made even worse by large lightmap scales however

VRAD appears to only sample the center of each lightmap texel, leading to poor quality
