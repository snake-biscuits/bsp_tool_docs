## 3D Visualiser
Over the past few months I've been building towards remapping lightmap uvs to a Lightmap Page
Today, I have succeeded: [bsp_tool_examples/valve/source_lightmap_pages.py](https://github.com/snake-biscuits/bsp_tool_examples/blob/master/valve/source_lightmap_pages.py)

**NOTE**: At time of writing, there are a few bugs:
 * Faces w/ T-junctions have broken uvs
 * Lots of manual cleanup is required (separate `toolsblockshadow`, use `Lightmap` material etc.)

I'm using a similar setup to what I did with `mp_corporate.gltf`
Using Shader AOVs in Blender to examine each lightmap
Though this time I'm using a single shader for the whole map
Should make it easy to composite the lightmaps together in 3D too

Couldn't get away with this on a Titanfall Engine map since there's multiple of each lightmap
Blending lightmaps with `.vmt` shaders & comparing that to in-game will still be useful tho
