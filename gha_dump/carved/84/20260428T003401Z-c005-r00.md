Some portal planes are non-axial
This is why `CM_BRUSH_SIDE_PLANE_OFFSETS` can be so convoluted
Occasionally a `BrushSide` will lie on the same plane as a portal, so a negative index is used

`BrushSide` `Plane` indexing is really wierd
All axial sides of a brush are handled by the **Axis-Aligned** Bounding Box of that brush
Since any axial side of a convex hull cannot have any point outside that side
Meaning you don't have to store those 6 essential `Planes` for each `Brush`
Which is why all `Planes` in the `Brush` section of the lump are all non-axial
