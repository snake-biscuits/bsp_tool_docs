Understanding `PortalVertexEdges` really helped crack the whole system wide open
`IndexSets` exist because a given `PortalVertex` can be on multiple `PortalEdges`
Each one has to be on at least 2, as it's a "corner" of a `Portal` winding

**But what's really crucial is that there's more than 2 vertices on each edge!**
However, `PortalEdges` only index the `PortalVertices` at the extreme edges of each "edge group"
Which is why all our attempts to visualise `PortalEdges` as **polygons** were such messes

`PortalEdges` are **edges**, and those edges can be longer that the segments which touch a `Portal`
Once you accept this, it's easier to understand the `PortalVertexEdges` & `PortalEdgeIntersect*` lumps
