`PortalEdges` are a little bit messier than intially thought

By far the most confusing to reverse, `PortalEdges` functions unlike any other lump.
It contains pairs of indices, and either member of that pair in indexed individually.
(at least, when the index if coming from `PortalEdgeRefs`)

There are twice as many entries in `PortalEdges` as `PortalEdgeIntersectHeaders`.
Meaning they are parallel, when you recognise that a pair of indices is one `PortalEdge`.

But `PortalEdges` can be shared across multiple `Portals`.
Even though each `PortalEdge` only has a start & end point.
We aren't constrained by the bounds of the `Portal` "winding" (polygon)
So edges can actually encapsulate multiple points which occur on that edge.

`PortalVertexEdges` & `PortalEdgeIntersect*` lumps map these edge-vertex relationships.
`PortalEdges` indexes `PortalVertices`, but only the vertices at the extreme ends.

There's a much more detailed breakdown in `bsp_tool_docs`: [`Titanfall Portal System`](https://github.com/snake-biscuits/bsp_tool_docs/blob/main/docs/branches/respawn/titanfall/portals.md)
