[`func_viscluster`](https://developer.valvesoftware.com/wiki/Func_viscluster) is an internal entity[^int_ent] which can be used to control the grouping of visclusters.

Interestingly, it selects lumps in vertical chunks.
Due to gravity<sup>[citation needed]</sup>, a lot of games are actually optimised w/ vertical groupings
See:
 * Titanfall 2 `CM_GRID_CELLS`
 * Minecraft Chunks

The Source Engine `VVIS` compiler also automatically generates visleaves of extreme height in a regular grid.
IIRC this is because in an large open field you need to break the space up into leaves for effective frustrum culling.
Other VIS compilers may also do this, I haven't checked.

[^int_ent]: [Valve Developer Community](https://developer.valvesoftware.com/wiki/Main_Page): [Internal Entities](https://developer.valvesoftware.com/wiki/Category:Internal_entities)
