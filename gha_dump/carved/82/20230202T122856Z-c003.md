Portal ([the game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Game_(mind_game))) is also a very useful lens to talk about portals through (as are lenses tbh).
A limited opening through which you can see another space, these spaces need not be connected in reality.

When creating a "realistic" space, it can be helpful for portals to connect adjacent spaces.
But when they don't you can create [Non-Euclidean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-Euclidean_geometry) spaces[^no_euc][^p2_noneuc]

Most *Quake-based BSP Engines* keep their levels Euclidean
Portal 2 is an exception, using "World Portals"[^vdc_lpd][^thw_wp] you can somewhat seamlessly connect spaces in otherwise impossible ways.
