The Architectural definition of Portal is actually the most useful to us.
It describes how Visleaves & Portals interact in relatively common **human terms**

Leaves are rooms, and portals are where light enters (and exits!) each room.

Portals describe the connections between spaces.

DOOM & Quake are probably the best examples for discussing their basis.
In DOOM, sectors are areas which can be drawn, and portals function as a sort of stencil buffer.
See the Bisqwit & 3DSage videos cited above for a more technical explanation.
