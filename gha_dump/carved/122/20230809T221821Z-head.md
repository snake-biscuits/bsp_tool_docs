The Apex Legends change to `.bsp_lump`-only lumps means some of RTL's data is lost (RTL-C)
`LIGHTMAP_DATA_UNKNOWN` might be **some** of the extra texels

**IT IS NOT `RTL.C`**, they co-exist before the switch to `.bsp_lump` only

`LIGHTMAP_DATA_UNKNOWN` is always 4/13ths of `RTL.length` & 1/3 of `RTL.filesize`

`LIGHTMAP_DATA_UNKNOWN.length` + `RTL.filesize` != `RTL.length`

> RTL is the only lump where `.bsp` & `.bsp_lump` sizes differ (9 vs 8 bytes per texel)

> NOTE: `LIGHTMAP_DATA_UNKNOWN` immediately preceeds `LIGHTMAP_DATA_SKY` in the lump order
