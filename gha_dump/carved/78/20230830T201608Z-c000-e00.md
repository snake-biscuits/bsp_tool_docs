# Timeline of Titanfall modding

## 2014
### January
Before Titanfall's retail release, but after the public betas and tech tests, [Cra0kalo](https://github.com/cra0) makes plans to create modding tools[^1]
### February
[Cra0kalo](https://github.com/cra0) reverses Respawn's `.vpk` archive format and publicly releases tools (closed source) to extract their files[^2]
### March
By early March `.bsp` files had been dug into, with strings identifying multiple lump names found in `bin/x64_retail/bsppack.dll`
[ata4](https://github.com/ata4) starts work on bringing Titanfall "support" to [bspsrc](https://github.com/ata4/bspsrc), a popular Source Engine map decompiler[^3]
### August
[Cra0kalo](https://github.com/cra0) theorises (incorrectly) that the map format hasn't changed much since Portal 2[^4]

## 2016
Titanfall 2 releases

## 2017
### May
The Titanfall 2 single-player only [Icepick](https://titanfallmods.com/) mod launcher enters development[^5]

## 2020
### March
[snake-biscuits](https://github.com/snake-biscuits) is killed by a CS:GO hacker with a YouTube URL for a name; they follow this link, and find cra0's blog
Discovering VPK Tool, they add `mods/titanfall2.py` to [bsp_tool](https://github.com/snake-biscuits/bsp_tool)[^6]

Various projects to create modded clients for Titanfall: Online, Titanfall 2 & Apex Legends cross paths across multiple Discord servers
[snake-biscuits](https://github.com/snake-biscuits) gets involved, trying to port maps between all 3 games

## 2021
### August
The Apex Legends mod client [R5Reloaded](https://r5reloaded.com/) releases to the public[^7]
### December
[BobTheBob](https://github.com/BobTheBob9) releases `v1.0.0` of the Titanfall 2 Northstar client publically, just in time for Christmas[^8]

## 2022
### April
[F1F7Y](https://github.com/F1F7Y) begins work on [MRVN-Radiant](https://github.com/MRVN-Radiant/MRVN-Radiant), a fork of the [NetRadiant-custom](https://github.com/Garux/netradiant-custom) level editing software[^9]

### June
[barnabwhy](https://github.com/barnabwhy) releases their open source VPK tool: [TFVPKTool](https://github.com/barnabwhy/TFVPKTool)[^10] (Cra0's tool was closed source)
This forms the backend for [HarmonyVPKTool](https://github.com/harmonytf/HarmonyVPKTool)

By the end of the year [MRVN-Radiant](https://github.com/MRVN-Radiant/MRVN-Radiant) is compiling basic maps for all 3 games
Momentum researching the `.bsp` format continues to grow exponentially, with better and better tools helping us to experiment & learn
