In 2023, we have a pretty firm grasp on how Respawn Source renders `.bsp` files
We're really starting to dig into the Visibility (culling, occlusion, depth sorting etc.) systems, as well as physics

Map decompilers are good enough to be educational, and we have some understanding of how to get lighting in-game

A lot of our development has been focused on Titanfall 2, but we can test maps on all 3 games (including the currently private TF:O client)
We've ported TF:O's mp_box to Titanfall 2 & helped the R5Reloaded team create World's Edge: After Dark

There's a lot of big ideas ahead, but we're still slowly building up the tools
Very happy to be at the point where progress is something that can be expressed in screenshots and videos
